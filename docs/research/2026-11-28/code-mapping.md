**5 highest-value gaps given the current code**

1. **F – court- or hitter-relative hit features** (`events/learned.py:89-92`). This is the documented cause of the DJI_0009 regression (learned v2 34/58 vs rules 47/58). The pipeline already has the hitter's court position; it needs a feature swap, retraining, and the adoption rule in `PLAN.md` step B.
2. **E – swing evidence inside the hit scorer, plus one hit per swing** (`hits.py:190-210` using `pose_hits.swing_curve`). The swing model already exists but only adds hits and is off by default. Using it to reject trajectory-only false hits (pickups, self-feeds, tracker noise on amateur footage) is untested.
3. **C + B – occlusion augmentation, visibility-aware loss, and a blur-midpoint labelling rule** (`training.py:386-396`, `:185-194`, `label_tool.py`). Cheap. The shuttle README puts side-court coverage at about 50% and says lowering thresholds doesn't help, so fine-tuning is the lever. A visibility-weighted loss first needs visibility classes in the label schema.
4. **L – refine far-player pose with a top-down pass** (`pose.py:266-267`, `runner.py:148-153`). The far player is missing in 23% of DJI frames (`events/config.py:42-45`), and the far-half pass never improves a poorly detected far player who was already found. This affects who gets named as hitter, foot positions, and stroke features.
5. **N – serve legality on the learned stroke model, used as a rally-split signal** (`strokes/classify.py:76-84`). Small change. A mid-rally "serve" should flag merged rallies (a known failure in `PLAN.md` step C) rather than just being masked. The quick calibration fix (O: snap the web's manual corners to the court lines at `app.py:476`) is equally small and worth doing alongside.
